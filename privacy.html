Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

StreamPro is an independent TV activation and information resource. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

We do not collect payment information or TV account credentials. We cannot access subscriber databases for any TV provider.

How We Use It

  • To respond to questions and activation enquiries via the contact form

Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings.

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with our hosting provider under appropriate agreements.

Your Rights

Email privacy@streampro.com to access, correct, or delete your data. We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes
